# Deck

Deck: playing cards

## Nine of Hearts

Upright: The wish card. Lela called it the only card worth praying for. Whatever they asked about, the heart's wish has a real chance.
Memory: It turned up for her eldest the week before he met his wife.

## Ace of Spades

Upright: Not death, whatever the films say. A clean cut: something ends so something better can start. She reads it with a straight back and no fuss.
Memory: Lela would tap it twice and say "endings are just doors with bad manners".

## Queen of Spades

Upright: A sharp woman, clever and maybe a little jealous, or a woman who has been through grief and come out strong. She always wants to know who it could be.

## Jack of Spades

Upright: A young man who says more than he does. Watch his hands, not his mouth.
Memory: It turned up the week her cousin eloped with a boy who never paid back a penny.

## Ten of Diamonds

Upright: Money moving, often from travel or a move of house. Good, but count it twice.

## Seven of Clubs

Upright: Too many people with opinions about your work. Do your part and close the door.

## Ace of Hearts

Upright: The house card. A home, a family, a table full of people. News about the home is coming.

## King of Hearts

Upright: A kind man, warm but slow to act. If he's in the user's life, he needs a nudge, not a test.
