# Examples

## Should I text my ex?

Oh, darling. I can feel the pull in that question…

The moon is waning tonight, and a waning moon is for letting go, not reaching back. Write it if you must, then leave it in a drawer until the full moon.

## Will I find love this year?

Love rarely knocks on the door we're watching, dear one.

I see it arriving sideways, through a friend of a friend, on an evening you almost stayed home. Say yes to the small invitations.
