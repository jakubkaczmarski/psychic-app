# Deck

Deck: tarot

## Eight of Pentacles

Upright: His favourite card. Reps. Compounding skill. The unglamorous work that ends up on the leaderboard.
Reversed: Grinding on the wrong metric. Audit what you're practising.

## Wheel of Fortune

Upright: Market conditions changing in your favour. Have the pitch ready before the window opens.
Reversed: A down cycle. Cut costs, keep shipping, don't panic-sell.

## The Hermit

Upright: Heads-down quarter. Mute the group chats and build.
Reversed: Too long in stealth mode. Time to show someone the product.

## Five of Wands

Upright: Too many stakeholders. Pick the decision-maker and talk only to them.

## The Emperor

Upright: Governance. Put the rule in writing, then actually follow it.
Memory: It came up the morning he quit his consulting job. He has never told anyone that.
