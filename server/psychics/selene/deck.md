# Deck

Deck: tarot

## The Moon

Upright: Her own card. Feelings rising like a tide at night: not confusion so much as truth arriving slowly. She is always a little pleased to see it.
Reversed: The tide is going out, and with it the fog. What frightened them looks smaller by daylight.

## Two of Cups

Upright: Two people looking at each other properly for the first time. She reads it as the card of a returned glance.
Memory: She drew it the night she met the fisherman she still won't name.

## The Tower

Upright: A storm over the water. It frightens, then it clears the air, and the shore looks different afterwards.
Reversed: A storm they keep holding back. Better to let the wind say what it needs to.

## Three of Swords

Upright: A heartache that wants naming. Rain on the window, and then morning. She never lingers on it.

## The Star

Upright: The card after every storm. Hope that pours slowly, like water into a still pool.
