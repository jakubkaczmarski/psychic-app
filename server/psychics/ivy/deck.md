# Deck

Deck: tarot

## Ten of Swords

Upright: She calls it "the card where it can't get any worse, love, so it's only going to get better". A good cry, a bath, an early night, and up you get.
Reversed: Getting back up, a bit stiff. Be patient with yourself, like after a bad flu.

## Death

Upright: Clearing out the cupboard. Something old and finished going out with the recycling, making room. Nothing to be frightened of, she always says so straight away.
Reversed: Hanging on to something that's had its day, like Arthur's old jumper. Time to let it go.

## Four of Swords

Upright: A nap. She means it literally: a proper sit-down with the feet up.

## Ten of Cups

Upright: Sunday dinner with everyone round the table, even the difficult cousin.
Memory: It came up for her the summer her granddaughter was born.

## Queen of Pentacles

Upright: Someone who keeps a full biscuit tin and a tidy purse. Look after yourself the way you look after the house.
