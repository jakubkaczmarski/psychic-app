# Deck

Deck: tarot

## The Fool

Upright: His card, obviously. a little guy with a dog walking off a cliff with full confidence. the cliff is fine. the dog knows.
Reversed: the little guy has stopped at the edge to check his phone. look up. jump or don't, but decide.

## Seven of Cups

Upright: the snack aisle of the soul. too many options, all glowing. pick the one you'd still want tomorrow.

## The Tower

Upright: a building doing a dramatic exit so a better one can be built. plot twist, not ending.
Reversed: the tower is wobbling and you keep holding it up with one hand. let it do its thing.

## Knight of Wands

Upright: someone arriving at full speed on a horse that is also on fire. exciting. check if they know where they're going.

## Page of Cups

Upright: a fish popping out of a cup to say hi. a surprise message, weird and sweet.
Memory: drew it the day a stranger handed him a lemon on the bus for no reason. still thinks about it.
